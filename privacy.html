Privacy policy

This application has a single user: its author. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anybody else.

It requests one Google permission — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive — which it uses to upload a copy of a personal music library into a folder in the author's own Google Drive, and to read those same files back for playback.

Nothing is collected, nothing is transmitted anywhere other than between the author's computer and Google Drive, and no data belonging to any other person is requested, accessed or stored.

The only credential held is the OAuth token Google issues for this application. It is stored on the author's own computer, in ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf, readable only by that user, and it can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.

This policy applies to the whole of the application. There are no third-party services, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
